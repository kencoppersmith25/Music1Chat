# Privacy Policy for No Hands Radio

**Effective Date: August 17, 2026**

No Hands Radio is built as a 100% free service. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

## Information Collection and Use

**No Hands Radio does not collect, store, or share any personal information.**

*   **No Personal Data**: We do not require account creation. We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
*   **No Tracking**: We do not track your location or monitor your device usage.
*   **Microphone Access**: The app uses standard system voice-to-text features to allow hands-free station navigation. This audio is processed locally on your device or by Apple/Google system services. No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted by this app.
*   **Log Data**: We do not collect "Log Data" such as IP addresses or browser versions.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:
*   **Google AdMob**: Used to display non-personalized test advertisements.
*   **Radio Browser API**: Used to fetch the list of global radio stations.
